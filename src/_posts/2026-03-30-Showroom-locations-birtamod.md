Locations are ordered according to proximity with Mukti chowk. All places are next to the Asian highway

## The 12-shutter centepede (500-1000 sq feet)

[Show in maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CMYVxgiAy7dK1g8L6)

<img src="/images/IMG_0407.jpeg" alt="">
<video controls src="/images/output.mp4"></video>

### Pros
- Very close to Mukti chowk. Largest footfall
- Right next to a petrol pump
- Large space, will need to demolish walls. 600-1000 sq feet
- Can store inventory and service center in the same place

### Cons
- Will need to take up 12 shutters. Can sub let.
- Highest rent price - 1L/month
